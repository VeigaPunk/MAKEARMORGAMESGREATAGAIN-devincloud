THE ARENA CHRONICLE

The gladiator’s handbook

Attack trades reliability for damage. Shield breaker is stronger, braces against 2 damage, and recovers for two actions. Guard blocks 8 damage; each opponent has a distinct heavy-strike rhythm, shown before your action. Potions restore 26 HP and brace against 3 damage.

Your six skill points affect damage, accuracy, health, and defense. Wins grant gold and XP. Level ups improve health and damage. The smith equips purchases immediately. Health and two potions refill before every bout, even after defeat.

Mouse or touch to play. During combat: 1 Attack · 2 Shield breaker · 3 Potion · 4 Guard. Progress saves automatically between bouts.
